Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Apartment Hunter AI is a personal, non-commercial tool for monitoring apartment listings.

Google User Data

The application requests read-only access to Gmail to identify and process apartment listing notification emails.

How Data Is Used

Relevant emails are processed to extract apartment listing information, filter listings, prevent duplicates, and send matching alerts to the user's configured Telegram chat.

Storage and Sharing

The application stores information needed to process listings and prevent duplicate alerts. Gmail credentials and application data are intended to remain in private, access-controlled storage. Relevant listing details are sent to Telegram for notification purposes.

Data Retention and Deletion

Stored application data may be deleted by the application owner. Access to Gmail may be revoked through Google Account settings.

Access

The application is intended for use by its owner and is not offered as a public service.
